New to the dashboard?

The dashboard guide explains every section, what the buttons do, and how to save a favorite.

Favorites are your personal saved numbers. A Saved nodes dropdown, if present, is a separate preset list. Saving a favorite does not add it to that dropdown.

Read the dashboard guide →

Three things to try

  1. Check your callsign and node number. Make sure they match the station you want to monitor.
  2. Find BlueNode Controls. Enter a remote node and choose Connect. Choose Disconnect when you want to leave it.
  3. Save a favorite. Favorites and Recent Nodes give you shortcuts for the next visit. Bookmark the dashboard in your browser too.

You can leave the extras for later

Weather, Internet remote access, and automatic recovery are optional. Fresh guided installations leave automatic recovery off. BlueNode starts automatically after reboot.

Home-network access is for a trusted network. Before making the dashboard available over the Internet, follow the authenticated remote-access guide.

Help improve BlueNode

Please report any problems during installation, after installation, or while using the dashboard. Even a small issue or confusing step is useful feedback.

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and any error message. Include your BlueNode version and node hardware if you know them. Review screenshots and troubleshooting reports before posting; leave out passwords and other private details. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account.
